Human Machine Interaction Research Group

I am currently involved in a research collaboration within the Human Machine Interaction (HMI) research group at Universitas Ma Chung. The group focuses on developing assistive technologies and intelligent interaction systems that bridge humans and machines. One of the key research directions includes the development of accessibility devices such as adaptive computer input systems for individuals with physical disabilities. The goal is to design technology that enhances usability, inclusivity, and real-world human-computer interaction.
